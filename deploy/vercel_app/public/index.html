Under the hood

Production-grade meshes, not noise.

Quad-dominant topology

Edge flow you can subdivide, retopo, or rig — not the soup-mesh you get from most generators.

Clean UV layouts

Every mesh ships with a 1024² atlas, low distortion, manifold-checked. Drop straight into Substance.

Real formats

GLB, OBJ, STL, FBX — embedded textures, Y-up or Z-up. Configurable in the export panel.

~14s average

Most prompts return in fifteen seconds. Image conditioning adds a few. Studio plan runs uncapped.

Same-seed refinement

Iterate on a generation without losing what worked. "A little squatter, flared base" — preserved seed.

Wireframe, UV, stats

Inspect topology, UV islands, and material counts at any point. Editor surfaces the numbers that matter.
