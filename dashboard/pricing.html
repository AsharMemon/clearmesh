Frequently asked

What counts as a generation?

One prompt or one image-to-mesh run equals one generation, regardless of how many variations come back. Refinements at the same seed are billed at a quarter credit.

Can I use Clearmesh meshes commercially?

Yes, starting on Pro. Free-tier meshes are licensed for personal and non-commercial use; upgrade any time and the license retroactively covers everything in your library.

Do unused credits roll over?

Pro and Studio plans roll over up to one month of unused generations. Heavy weeks balance against quieter ones without forcing a higher tier.

How does the Blender bridge work?

An installable add-on that lets you open the Studio inside Blender, drop meshes straight into your scene, and re-prompt without leaving your viewport.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the next cycle, annual plans are prorated. Your library and exports stay accessible on the free tier afterwards.

Is there an academic discount?

Verified students and accredited educators get Pro at 50% off and Studio at 30% off. Email edu@clearmesh.app.
